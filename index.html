AKI Draw - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Crystalarts Industries ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the AKI Draw mobile application handles information when you use our app.

1. Photos and Images

AKI Draw provides photo editing features such as Pencil Sketch, Outline, Color Boost, and Anime Style. Image editing is performed locally on your device. We do not operate our own server to store your edited images.

2. Background Removal

The Background Removal feature opens the CleanBG third-party service. When you choose to use this feature, you may upload an image to CleanBG for processing. Your image will be handled according to the privacy policy and practices of that third-party service.

3. Advertisements

AKI Draw uses Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements to users of the free version of the app. Google AdMob may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and ad interaction information for advertising and related purposes, subject to Google's policies.

4. Saving Images

Images that you choose to save are stored on your device. AKI Draw does not upload saved images to our own servers.

5. Third-Party Services

AKI Draw uses third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob - for advertisements.
  • CleanBG - for background removal.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We do not operate our own server to collect or store your edited photographs. However, information processed by third-party services is subject to their respective privacy and security practices.

7. Children's Privacy

AKI Draw is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
